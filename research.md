# research.md — what is sourced, what is estimated

Updated 2026-10-14 (second pass). "S" = found in a source this session. "A" = source is an advocacy group or a consumer page, so treat with care. "E" = my estimate, **not verified**.

## Scale
- S: 87.9 billion land animals slaughtered in 2024 (FAOSTAT via Faunalytics). Ducks 4.2 billion. https://faunalytics.org/global-animal-slaughter-statistics-and-charts/
- S: about 31 billion land animals alive at any moment; about 74% factory-farmed (Sentience Institute via Our World in Data). https://ourworldindata.org/how-many-animals-are-factory-farmed
- E: birth weights in `W_REAL` (billions/year): broiler 66, layer-female 5, layer-male 5, duck 4.2, turkey 0.65, pig 1.5, sheep 0.6, beef 0.2, dairy-female 0.06, dairy-male 0.11, rabbit 0.4. Totals match FAO; the split is mine.

## Broiler chicken
- S: growth about 25 → 100 g/day in 50 years; slaughter weight in under 40 days (FAO). Leg disorders 9–33%; foot-pad dermatitis 22–79%; UK survey 27.6% walking problems, 3.3% almost unable to walk; ascites 4.7%; sudden death 3%; mortality 4.1–7.1%.
- S: EFSA 2014 — water-bath stunning did not demonstrate absence of pain before unconsciousness.
- E: slaughter 42 ± 3 days (fast), 56–81 (slower); catching and transport details.

## Laying hen
- S: males killed on day one; about 300 eggs/year; keel fractures up to 97% non-caged, ~85% Danish study; EU banned barren cages 2012; enriched cages ≥750 cm².
- S/A: slaughter at 72–78 weeks (rehoming charity, industry adviser: "72/76 weeks"); extended lay to 108 weeks with induced moult exists. App uses 75 ± 3.5 weeks.
- S: ~84.2% of laying hens worldwide are in cages, 12.4% indoor cage-free, 3.4% outdoor (WATTAgNet; the article date is unclear). The app splits "caged" into conventional 60 / enriched 24 — **that split is E**.
- A: spent-hen handling — broken bones common; one advocacy report says up to 50% of a flock arrive with fractures and 1–2% dead. Unverified method.
- E: beak-trimming rate (80%); feather-pecking rates; per-system mortality (8–10%).

## Pig
- S: 12–13 piglets/litter; weaning ~21 days; nursery to 2–3 months; slaughter 22–28 weeks, ~280 lb; gilt bred 170–220 days; gestation 114 days; farrowing crate 6–7 days before birth.
- S (new): pre-weaning mortality ≈ 12–15% in US databases (app: 14%). Sow annual replacement ≥ 50%; mean parity at cull 4.2 (US) / 4.8 (Spain); annual sow deaths 9.5–12.3% (PigCHAMP 2019). App: parity 4.2 ± 1.6; 4.5% death per parity (≈ 11%/year); 4% of females kept as gilts.
- S: tail-docking illegal as routine in EU; Swedish castration rules; stun-failure signs 3.2–12.5% (field study).
- E: 2.4 litters/year; the 12–13 piglet weight; system mix.

## Dairy cow and dairy male calf
- S: calves separated within minutes or hours; first calving ~2 years; total life 4.5–6 years; natural 15–20; ~57% of culls involuntary (FAWC).
- S (new): lameness — British meta-analysis: prevalence 29.5%, incidence 30.9 per 100 cow-years. App: p = .31 per lactation (.20 on pasture is E).
- S (new): clinical mastitis — 57-study review: 13–40 cases per 100 cow-years; Dutch estimate 32.5. App: p = .30 per lactation.
- S (new): pre-weaning heifer-calf mortality 6.5% (NAHMS 2014 preliminary, via a secondary source); 7.8–8.4% in earlier surveys; highest in the first 21 days. App: 6.5%.
- S (new): disbudding — in a Canadian survey 60% did it at 3–8 weeks, 28% before 3 weeks; hot-iron wounds heal in ~9 weeks and pain lasts ≥3 weeks; local anaesthetic plus NSAID is best practice.
- A: male calves "sent to slaughter at a very young age". E: bobby age (5–14 days), veal ~170 days, beef-on-dairy ~560 days; system mix.

## Beef cattle
- S: feeder calves arrive at 5–12 months; days on feed 120–300; BRD in 16.2% of cattle placed (USDA survey), range 8–52% across studies; BRD is ~75% of feedlot morbidity and 50–70% of mortality; liver abscesses 12–32%; beef-on-dairy 7.6–20%+ (Kansas State). 13.5% insufficient-stun signs with captive bolt.
- E: slaughter ~450 days (feedlot, ≈ entry at 210 + 240), 760 (pasture); branding rate.

## Sheep
- A: lamb mortality 9–20% worldwide, Australia 20–30%, >80% within 3 days (FOUR PAWS). Tail docking/castration by ring, often without anaesthetic (CIWF). Mulesing mainly Australia.
- S: heat stress major cause of deaths on live-export voyages (Carnovale & Phillips 2020). Lambs legally under 12 months; research ranges 4 / 6–7 / 12 months. App: ~7 months (pasture), 5 (feedlot).
- E: system mix; shearing; flystrike rate.
- Note: most sheep are on open pasture; the app says so.

## Duck
- S: foie gras force-feeding 11–15 days, twice daily, from day 63 or week 12; mortality 2–6% in force-feeding, 3.41% overall; 4.01 kg → 6.58 kg; ~37 million ducks and 700,000 geese a year in France.
- S (new): Pekin meat duck, European performance test: 3.6 kg at 42 days; 3 kg at 36 days; US hybrid slaughtered at 49 days. App: 43 ± 3 days.
- A: female mulard ducklings killed at hatch — Animal Law Europe estimates up to 40 million/year; footage from L214 (France); a French industry representative disputed that it is widespread. The app states the dispute.
- E: no open water in meat-duck sheds (generally reported; no source found this pass).

## Turkey (now sourced)
- S: hens ~12–14 weeks, toms up to 22 weeks (reviews); Norwegian study: toms slaughtered at 20 weeks, 14 kg carcass; National Turkey Federation: hens 14 weeks, toms ~18 weeks. App: toms 20 ± 1.5 weeks, hens 13.5 ± 1 week.
- S: EFSA — up to one third of the upper beak trimmed at hatch; toe trimming; desnooding in toms; all associated with acute and chronic pain; avoidable with better housing.
- S/A: HSUS report: industry tolerates mortality of 7–10% (2007, dated). App: ~8%.
- S/A: heavy breast prevents natural mating; artificial insemination is standard (industry and EFSA concerns).
- E: system mix (96% shed).

## Rabbit (now partly sourced)
- S: EFSA 2020 — slaughter at 63–77 days for light carcasses, up to 85–90 days in some systems; digestive disorders are the main cause of illness and death in growing rabbits from 3 weeks; welfare of growing rabbits is lowest in conventional cages of the six systems compared; stocking density > 40 kg/m² and slaughter after 10–11 weeks are hazards. EFSA 2022 transport: mean DOA 1.79% (breeding rabbits).
- E: mortality 14% in total (6% pre-weaning, 8% growing). EFSA gave no figure I could verify; a campaign claim of up to 30% could not be confirmed.
- E: 90% cage; the rest of the world's practice.

## Natural lifespans (all E, typical ranges)
Chicken 5–8 y; pig 15–20; cattle 15–20; sheep 10–12; duck 8–12; turkey ~10; rabbit 8–10.

## Still unverified (to check before sharing publicly)
Layer system split inside "caged"; sheep system mix; dairy system mix; the 4%-of-females gilt rate (derived, not sourced); the sow litter interval (145 days); veal and beef-on-dairy slaughter ages; Pekin "no open water"; rabbit mortality.

## Not in scope
Farmed fish; goats; geese; horses; breeding stock other than sows and dairy cows.

## Design notes
- No decisions. Animals do not choose.
- The four bars loosely follow the Five Domains idea. They are a model, not a measurement of feeling.
- Pauses fire at birth, midway and near the end.
- Every text is stored as an English + Japanese pair (`m(en, ja, ...args)`) and rendered at display time, so the language switch works in the middle of a life.
